# A splitter to turn one SAO ports into two

Expands a single Simple Add-On port on your conference badge into two ports for small SAOs.

Looks like a cute version of the smaller cousin of everyone's favorite anti-magic ray-slinging floating eyeball monster.

There's no power protection here, so only use small SAOs that won't use the full 250mA available from the source badge. Most SAOs don't use that much, so I figure this should be viable.

I've wanted to make a SAO splitter for a while. Deciding on the artistic form-factor was the hard part. I will claim I am partially inspired by the Beholder SAO I got at Supercon last year from Zachary Peterson (znjp), although since my KiCAD skills are more limited, I opted for a simpler design with fewer blinding LED beam attacks. Also, I'm in a rush and want this to work on the first try.
